Skill Sort- 2

Map how the skills build toward the target skill

Six skills feed into one target skill. Drag each card anywhere onto the open canvas and arrange them the way you think they build on one another. Then draw connectors: click a card’s small circle handle, then click the card it leads to, to draw an arrow between them. Click an arrow to remove it. One card doesn’t belong on this pathway at all; drag it to the doesn’t belong here zone instead of connecting it. Use each card’s “View practice problems” link as evidence for your thinking. When your group is ready, press Reveal the pathway to compare your map to the actual branch structure and discuss any differences.
